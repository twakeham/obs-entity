# Introduction

**Obsidian Entities** lets you decide what kinds of notes your vault holds — people, projects, tasks, meetings, books, characters, anything — and what each kind records about itself. Then it helps you work with them: create them from a form, link them together, see them in tables, boards, calendars and dashboards, and automate the repetitive parts.

Every entity is still an ordinary Markdown note. Its details live in the note's frontmatter, its links are wikilinks, and you can write whatever you like underneath. Switch the plugin off and your notes are all still there, readable by Obsidian and anything else.

## What it's for

You might use it to:

- **Manage work:** tasks with owners and due dates, meetings with attendees and actions, projects with budgets and risks.
- **Keep a CRM:** companies, contacts, deals in stages, and every call you've had.
- **Run a campaign:** sessions, characters, factions, quests and the places they happen.
- **Build a library:** books, authors, series and the quotes you've kept.

A ready-made **pack** gets you started on any of these in a click, and you can change everything afterwards. Or start empty and design your own — the [Getting started](/getting-started/) tutorial does exactly that.

## The pieces

| | |
|---|---|
| **Types** | A kind of note (Task, Person) with its fields, statuses, colour, icon and folder. Types sit in **groups**. |
| **Fields** | What a type records: text, numbers, dates, choices, checkboxes, lists, links to other entities — and **roll-ups** and **formulas** that are worked out for you. |
| **Relationships** | Link fields between types. A task's *Assignee* is a person; the person sees the task under *Tasks*. |
| **The properties form** | Replaces Obsidian's Properties block on entity notes with a form built from the type, so only valid values go in. |
| **Entity Browser** | A sidebar listing your entities by group and type, with your views and dashboards beside them. |
| **Views** | Saved tables, boards and calendars, with filters, sorting, grouping, totals and highlights. Each is a note. |
| **Dashboards** | Cards, charts, lists, buttons and filters on a grid. |
| **Workflows** | Automation: when something changes, when a note is created, from a button or a form, or on a schedule. |
| **Packs and apps** | Ways to share a set of types (and their views, templates and workflows), or your whole setup. |

[Concepts](./concepts) explains how these fit together.
