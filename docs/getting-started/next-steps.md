# Next steps

You've built a working task tracker and used every major part of the plugin. Here's where to go from here.

## Make it nicer to use

- **[Design a form](/guides/form-layouts):** arrange the task form in frames, columns and tabs, and show fields only when they apply.
- **[Validate what goes in](/guides/validation):** required fields, limits, patterns, and dates that must come after other dates.
- **[Smart defaults](/guides/defaults):** a due date a week out, a value copied from the linked meeting, task numbers that count up.
- **[Views inside notes](/guides/views-in-notes):** list a meeting's actions inside the meeting note, from its template.

## Automate more

- **[Workflow recipes](/guides/workflow-recipes):** buttons, forms, follow-up notes, reminders on dates and schedules.

## See a bigger example

- **[Tour: project management](/guides/project-management):** 22 types — a full RAID log, a backlog with sprints, governance and people — with dashboards that follow a project, a sprint or a person.
- **[Start from a pack](/guides/packs):** a CRM, a reading library, a research setup, a TTRPG campaign and more, ready to install and take apart.

## Look things up

The [Reference](/reference/notes) has every setting, field kind, formula function, view option, dashboard component, trigger and step.
